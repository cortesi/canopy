# Callouts

Obsidian's callouts are block quotes whose first line is `[!label]`, with an
optional fold sign and title. Any label in the common vocabularies opens
one, and takes the GitHub kind closest in meaning.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!tip] Pack light
> Take less.
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-tip">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">Pack light</p>
<p>Take less.</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

A fold sign may follow the label, and a label of another vocabulary takes
the closest kind.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!info]-
> body
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-note">
<p>body</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!faq]+ Why?
> Because.
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-warning">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">Why?</p>
<p>Because.</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

A callout may hold only its title, or nothing.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!tip] Title only
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-tip">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">Title only</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!danger]
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-caution">
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

GitHub's own kinds are callouts too.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!NOTE]
> GitHub's kind.
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-note">
<p>GitHub's kind.</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

An unknown label, or a title not set apart by a space, opens no callout.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!custom] x
> body
.
<blockquote>
<p>[!custom] x
body</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!tip]title
.
<blockquote>
<p>[!tip]title</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

Callouts nest, and a lazy line continues one.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!info] Outer
> > [!success] Inner
> > text
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-note">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">Outer</p>
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-tip">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">Inner</p>
<p>text</p>
</blockquote>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
> [!tip] T
lazy
.
<blockquote class="markdown-alert-tip">
<p class="markdown-alert-title">T</p>
<p>lazy</p>
</blockquote>
````````````````````````````````
