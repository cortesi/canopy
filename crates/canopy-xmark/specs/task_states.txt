# Task states

Beyond `[ ]` and `[x]`, a task list marker may hold any other visible
character, as Obsidian's themes and its Tasks plugin use them. Such a task
shows as done, marked with its state.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
- [ ] open
- [x] done
- [/] doing
- [-] dropped
.
<ul>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"/>
open</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox" checked=""/>
done</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox" checked="" data-task="/"/>
doing</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox" checked="" data-task="-"/>
dropped</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````

A bracket of two characters, or of a bracket, is no task.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
- [ab] text
- []] text
.
<ul>
<li>[ab] text</li>
<li>[]] text</li>
</ul>
````````````````````````````````
