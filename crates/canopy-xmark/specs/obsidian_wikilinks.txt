# Obsidian wikilinks and embeds

An unlabelled wikilink shows its target as Obsidian does: without `.md`,
and with its anchor after `›`. A label after `|` shows as written.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
[[Garden]], [[Projects/Garden.md]], [[Garden#Plan]], [[#^here]], and [[Garden|the garden]].
.
<p><a href="Garden">Garden</a>, <a href="Projects/Garden.md">Projects/Garden</a>, <a href="Garden#Plan">Garden › Plan</a>, <a href="#^here">^here</a>, and <a href="Garden">the garden</a>.</p>
````````````````````````````````

A wikilink image is an embed: of an image, by its extension, or of a note.
An image's label is its alt text, or its size in pixels.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
![[gradient.png]]

![[gradient.png|80]]

![[checker.png|48x48]]

![[gradient.png|A gradient]]

![[Garden#Plan]]
.
<p><img src="gradient.png" alt="gradient.png" /></p>
<p><img src="gradient.png" alt="gradient.png" width="80" /></p>
<p><img src="checker.png" alt="checker.png" width="48" height="48" /></p>
<p><img src="gradient.png" alt="A gradient" /></p>
<p><a class="internal-embed" href="Garden#Plan">Garden › Plan</a></p>
````````````````````````````````
