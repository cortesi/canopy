# Comments

Obsidian's comments, `%%…%%`, hide what they wrap, within a line or across
blocks.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
Read %%not this%% and this.
.
<p>Read and this.</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
%%Hidden%% opening.
.
<p>opening.</p>
````````````````````````````````

A comment can span blocks, and a paragraph it hides entirely shows nothing.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
Before.

%%
Hidden.

- hidden item
%%

After.
.
<p>Before.</p>
<p>After.</p>
````````````````````````````````

A comment can close in the middle of a block, and the rest of it shows.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
end of draft %%

hidden

still hidden %% Back to text.

After.
.
<p>end of draft</p>
<p>Back to text.</p>
<p>After.</p>
````````````````````````````````

A comment hides what it wraps before anything else is read, so a delimiter
inside it opens or closes nothing.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
a **b %%c** d%% e
.
<p>a **b e</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
**a %%b
c%% d**
.
<p><strong>a
d</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

Code and math show their markers, and a lone marker shows as written.

```````````````````````````````` example_xmark
`%%…%%` marks a comment, and so does $%%$.

```
%%
```

One %% alone.
.
<p><code>%%…%%</code> marks a comment, and so does <span class="math math-inline">%%</span>.</p>
<pre><code>%%
</code></pre>
<p>One %% alone.</p>
````````````````````````````````
